      How to try it

      1. Hold the Triki however is comfortable, then choose Preview. That angle becomes level, so the cursor stands still. Only the blue cursor in the test area moves; your real mouse is untouched.
      2. Tilt it to move: tilting right moves right, tilting away from you moves up. If the directions are turned, change Tilt direction.
      3. Twist it like a cap to scroll, and tap it with a finger to click (two taps double-click).
      4. Turn it face-down to pause; the LED goes off. When you pick it up again, your new grip becomes level, like lifting a mouse to re-grip it.
      5. When it feels right, choose Live to steer your real cursor in Chrome or any app. Keep this window open (minimised is fine); closing it turns the mouse off.
